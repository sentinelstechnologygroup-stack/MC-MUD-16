# MCMUD 16 Website Records Request

Prepared October 10, 2026 for the district website redesign. Request final, signed or certified records from the district secretary, attorney, tax assessor-collector and engineer as appropriate. The district's attorney should confirm the applicable posting requirements and approve the public versions. This is a website verification checklist, not a determination that the district has failed to meet a legal requirement.

## Message to send to the district

Subject: MCMUD 16 website update and supporting records

To complete the website updates and maintain an accurate public record, please provide the final district records supporting the current board roster, officer titles and updated tax rate, along with the approved contacts and policies listed below. Approved PDFs are preferred; please identify the adoption or approval date and effective date where applicable.

For the board updates, we need the appointment or election records establishing each director's service and the minutes or resolution establishing the current officer positions. For the $0.988 per $100 figure, please confirm the tax year, total adopted property-tax rate, component rates and effective date, and provide the signed tax-rate adoption order and related hearing notice. Please also identify any separate changes to water or sewer service rates.

We have received the MS4 authorization, utilities map, Storm Water Management Plan and two EPA brochures. Please confirm that these are the approved versions for public posting, and provide the designated stormwater reporting contact, accessibility contact and any adopted website policies. If a requested record does not apply or has been superseded, please identify the current controlling record. Please mark any material that should be retained for verification but not posted publicly.

## ADA accessibility and website policies

- Approved ADA nondiscrimination notice and the designated accessibility/ADA contact, if one has been designated. Obtain the public name or office, telephone, email and mailing address.
- Any adopted ADA grievance procedure, accommodation-request procedure and meeting accommodation instructions. If the district has no designated coordinator or adopted grievance procedure, ask counsel to confirm the procedure and wording appropriate to this district.
- District attorney's approval or edits to the draft ADA and Accessibility Notice, Privacy Notice and Terms of Use. Obtain the approval/adoption date and the date the notices should take effect.
- Confirm the final hosting provider, authentication provider, analytics/tracking configuration, email/alert provider and retention practices so the privacy notice matches the deployed website.
- Confirm the designated public-information request email/address and any adopted records-retention or privacy policies relevant to website communications and document publishing.
- Identify documents needing accessible replacement files, searchable text, tagging or an alternative format. Posting an ADA notice alone does not establish website or PDF accessibility.

## Existing website records to confirm

- Latest approved meeting minutes, all currently posted agendas and any special-meeting notices. Confirm the regular meeting schedule, location and accommodation instructions with the secretary; specific posted notices control individual meetings.
- Current election orders and notices, candidate filing information and deadlines approved for the applicable election. Preserve past election documents as historical records.
- Latest adopted budget, completed annual audit and the district attorney's approved financial and tax disclosures for website publication.
- Current district consultant/operator directory and approved public service contacts, including normal service requests and after-hours water or wastewater emergencies.
- Current extreme-weather/emergency preparedness notice, water-conservation information, and any applicable annual water-quality report or other notices the operator and attorney identify for this district.
- District attorney's confirmation of any additional statutory website postings applicable to MUD 16. Request existing approved records rather than creating a substitute for an official notice.

## Final publication package

For each public document, request its official title, date, signed/final PDF, whether it supersedes an earlier record, and the approved website section. Request a searchable and tagged accessible PDF where available. Keep the original official record; do not silently rewrite or alter it.

Ask the district to distinguish approved public files from records supplied only for verification. Personal addresses, private telephone numbers, identifiers and other confidential information should be reviewed by the district before publication. Obtain a named contact authorized to approve future website updates.
